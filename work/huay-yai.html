The Story

The Challenge

Huay Yai Park Villas — a new developer building 40 villas in a gated community in Pattaya's Huay Yai area — had zero online presence and a clear target: international buyers looking to invest in Thailand.

Our Approach

We built the entire online presence from scratch, starting with a lead-generating landing page covering everything a buyer needs to know, connected directly to WhatsApp so prospects could talk to the developer in real time. Social media followed, built around why to buy, what buyers actually get, and the surrounding Huay Yai area — giving foreign buyers the confidence to invest in a place they'd never physically seen.

The Result

200+ leads and 100+ WhatsApp conversations in just 2 months, with every lead qualified through CRM — handing the sales team a clean pipeline of real prospective buyers, not just names on a list.
