Content Production in Pattaya & Thailand

Unsocials Thailand · Pattaya · Bangkok

Content is the fuel of every marketing channel.

Ads, social media, websites and listings all run on visuals. Unsocials produces scroll-stopping photo and video content for brands across Thailand — from quick-turnaround reels to full brand films — with an in-house team in Pattaya.

What We Produce

Short-form video. Reels and TikToks built for the first three seconds — hook first, vertical first.

Brand films. Longer storytelling pieces for websites, launches and campaigns.

Photography. Rooms, food, venues, people, products and lifestyle imagery for social, websites, OTA listings and ads.

Ad creative. Multiple cuts and formats of the same shoot, so performance campaigns always have fresh variations to test.

Event coverage. Parties, launches and guest-chef nights captured and edited fast enough to post while the event is still being talked about.

Built For Short-Form

Thailand's audiences live on TikTok and Instagram Reels. We shoot vertical-first, hook-first content engineered for the first 3 seconds, then cut it into platform-ready variations for feed, stories, reels and ads.

One shoot day should produce weeks of content. We plan shot lists around your content calendar before the camera comes out, so nothing is filmed without a purpose.

Hospitality Specialists

Hotels, beach clubs, restaurants and villas are our home turf. We know how to make a room feel bigger without making it look fake, how to film a beach club so it feels busy and premium, and how to shoot food that holds up on a phone screen.

See the content behind Bamboo Beach Club, Nomads Hostel, Alexa Beach Club and Elysium Pattaya — every asset was produced in-house.

How A Shoot Works

Brief. What the content needs to do, where it will run, and who it's for.

Plan. Shot list, schedule, locations, talent and props — agreed before shoot day.

Shoot. Photo and video captured together, efficiently, with minimum disruption to your guests and staff.

Edit. Colour grading, sound, captions and platform-specific cuts.

Deliver. Organised, ready-to-post files, plus raw selects for your own use.

Volume Without Compromise

Monthly production retainers keep your feeds fed with consistent, on-brand assets — no more scrambling for content or managing freelancers. Where a shot is impossible or too expensive to film, we extend production with AI creative and CGI.

Who This Is For

Brands that need a constant stream of high-quality content without hiring a full in-house team. Especially hotels, resorts, beach clubs, restaurants, villas and real estate projects in Pattaya, Bangkok, Phuket and Krabi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you shoot?

Our team is based in Pattaya and shoots across Thailand, including Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi and Hua Hin. Travel outside Pattaya is quoted per project.

How long is a typical shoot?

Most social media shoots run two to four hours. Brand films and larger campaigns are planned as half-day or full-day productions.

Do you provide models?

We can source models and talent for a shoot. Talent fees are quoted separately from production.

How fast is delivery?

Social media edits are usually delivered within a week of the shoot. Brand films and larger edits depend on scope and are scheduled at the brief stage.

Can I use the content for ads and my website?

Yes. Content we produce for you is delivered for use across your social media, website, ads and listings.

Do you offer monthly content retainers?

Yes. Monthly retainers combine planned shoots, editing and AI-assisted content so your channels always have fresh material.
