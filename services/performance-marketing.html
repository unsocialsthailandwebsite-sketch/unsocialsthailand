01 · Performance Marketing

Performance Marketing in Pattaya & Thailand

Unsocials Thailand · Pattaya · Bangkok

We don't run ads. We build revenue systems.

Unsocials is a performance marketing agency based in Pattaya, working with hotels, restaurants, beach clubs, real estate developers and product brands across Thailand. Every baht of ad spend is tied to a number you can check: enquiries, WhatsApp conversations, bookings, sales.

What Performance Marketing Covers

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram). Full-funnel campaigns — reach, retargeting and conversion — with creative built for each stage, not one ad boosted to everyone.

Google Ads. Search, Performance Max and Google Hotel Ads, capturing people who are already looking for what you sell in Pattaya, Bangkok or Phuket.

TikTok Ads. For brands whose audience is young, domestic, or discovering where to go next on their phone.

Tracking and attribution. GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and Conversions API set up properly — including cross-domain tracking to booking engines, which most hotel sites in Thailand get wrong.

Landing pages and funnels. If the page an ad sends people to doesn't convert, the ad is wasted. We fix that side too.

Built For The Thai Market

Campaigns in Thailand need bilingual creative, local targeting logic and conversion paths that end in WhatsApp or LINE — not just a checkout page. A guest in Mumbai, a family in Bangkok and a retiree in Stockholm book in completely different ways.

So we split ad sets by market: Thai domestic, regional (India, China, Southeast Asia) and long-haul (Europe, Russia, Australia). Each gets its own creative, language, timing and budget. Relevance stays high, cost per result stays low.

We also plan around seasonality. High season and low season need different budgets, offers and audiences — spending the same amount every month is how ad budgets quietly leak.

How We Work

Audit. We review your current ad accounts, tracking and website conversion path. Most of the time, money is being lost before any new campaign is needed.

Tracking first. Nothing launches until we can measure it — from click to enquiry to booking.

Launch and test. Several audiences and creative angles at once, so the data picks the winners instead of opinions.

Scale what works. Budget moves weekly toward the ads, audiences and placements producing results.

Report in plain numbers. Monthly reporting on spend, cost per enquiry and return — not impressions and likes.

Any brand spending money on ads that isn't seeing a clear, trackable return. Hotels paying 15–25% OTA commission on every booking. Developers who need qualified buyer leads, not form spam. Restaurants and beach clubs that need footfall on weeknights, not just weekends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I spend on ads in Thailand?

It depends on your market and goal. We recommend a test budget large enough to get clear data within the first month, then scaling what works. Ad spend is always paid directly to Meta, Google or TikTok and is separate from our management fee.

Do you manage Google Hotel Ads?

Yes. We set up and manage Google Hotel Ads alongside Meta Ads, with tracking connected to your booking engine so you can see which channel produces direct bookings.

Can you track bookings that happen on WhatsApp or LINE?

Yes. We set up click-to-WhatsApp and LINE tracking and conversion events so chat enquiries are measured as results, not lost between platforms.

Do you run ads in Thai and English?

Yes. Ad sets are split by audience, and creative is written in the language that audience books in — Thai for domestic guests, English and other languages for international markets.

Do you guarantee bookings or sales?

No agency can honestly guarantee bookings — those depend on your product, pricing and service. We are accountable for building, running and optimising campaigns and tracking, and we report every result transparently.

How quickly will I see results?

Campaigns generate data within the first week. Most clients see a clear picture of cost per enquiry within the first month, and performance improves as we optimise over the following two to three months.
