AI Creative Automation in Thailand

Unsocials Thailand · Pattaya · Bangkok

Unsocials pioneered AI creative automation for the Thai market.

Custom-trained AI models generate studio-quality visual content for your brand in hours, not weeks. Not generic stock-looking AI images — visuals trained on your property, products and brand style, then directed and finished by our creative team.

What AI Creative Automation Means

Custom-trained models. We train AI on your real photography — your rooms, dishes, products or spaces — so new visuals stay accurate to what customers will actually experience.

Virtual brand ambassadors. Consistent AI characters who represent your brand across posts, reels and ads.

Ad variations at scale. Dozens of visual variations for testing, produced in a fraction of the time of a reshoot.

Concept and campaign visuals. Seasonal campaigns, launches and ideas visualised before you spend on production.

Human direction. Every output is art-directed, retouched and checked by our team. AI is the tool, not the creative director.

Virtual Brand Ambassadors

Our proprietary AI representatives Ava & Alex shoot every day, never cancel, and cost a fraction of influencer rates. We can build the same for your brand — a dedicated virtual ambassador trained on your style, wearing your merchandise, sitting in your restaurant or relaxing at your pool.

Infinite Ad Variations

Test 50 visual variations of a room, dish or product in minutes. Business travellers see workspaces; couples see candle-lit views; families see the kids' pool. Higher relevance, higher conversion — and much faster learning in your ad campaigns.

Where It Works Best

Hotels and resorts — seasonal and audience-specific visuals without reshooting rooms.
Restaurants and beach clubs — event, menu and campaign visuals on tight timelines.
Real estate — lifestyle imagery for projects that are still being built (often combined with CGI).
Fashion, jewellery and products — concept carousels and model visuals without a full studio day.

AI Plus Real Production

AI doesn't replace a real shoot — it multiplies it. We usually combine one well-planned content shoot with AI-generated extensions, so you get the authenticity of real footage and the volume of AI. For cinematic video, see AI film production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will AI content look fake?

Not when it is done properly. We train models on your real photography and every output is art-directed and retouched by our team. Content that doesn't meet the standard doesn't get published.

Is AI-generated content safe to use for my brand?

Yes. We create original visuals rather than copying anyone else's work, and we keep AI visuals accurate to your real product so customers are never misled.

What is a virtual brand ambassador?

A consistent AI character created for your brand who appears across your posts, reels and ads — like an influencer you own, available every day.

Do I still need a photo shoot?

Usually one good shoot is the best starting point. Real photography trains the AI and keeps your content authentic; AI then multiplies it into far more variations.

How fast can you deliver AI content?

Once a model is trained on your brand, new visuals and variations can be produced within days rather than weeks.
