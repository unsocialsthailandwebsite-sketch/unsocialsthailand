02 · Social Media

Social Media Management in Pattaya & Thailand

Unsocials Thailand · Pattaya · Bangkok

Your social feed is your storefront.

Before anyone books your hotel, visits your beach club or enquires about your villa, they check your Instagram. Unsocials manages social media end-to-end for hospitality, real estate and lifestyle brands across Thailand — strategy, production, posting and community management.

What's Included

Strategy. A clear content direction built around what makes your brand different — not a template every venue in Pattaya is already using.

Monthly content calendar. Planned around your commercial goals: events, seasons, offers and launches. Every reel, story and post has a job — reach, bookings, or brand authority.

Content production. Reels, carousels, static posts and stories produced by our in-house team. See content production.

Posting and scheduling. Platform-native formats, captions and hashtags, published at the times your audience is actually online.

Community management. Comments and DMs handled, enquiries routed to your team on WhatsApp or LINE.

Monthly reporting. Reach, engagement, follower growth and — most importantly — enquiries generated.

Platforms We Manage

Instagram is the main discovery and booking-decision platform for hospitality in Thailand. TikTok drives reach, especially with domestic and younger audiences. Facebook still matters for Thai audiences, events and community. LinkedIn for B2B, real estate and founder-led brands.

We don't spread you across every platform for the sake of it. Strategy decides where you need to win.

Bilingual By Default

Thai and English content flows are built in — so you win both the domestic market and international visitors. Thai captions are written for Thai readers, not machine-translated afterwards.

Why Most Venue Feeds Don't Work

Most hotels and beach clubs in Thailand post the same sunset, the same pool shot and the same template graphics. It's pleasant and completely forgettable. The feeds that grow build a recognisable world: a point of view, recurring formats, real people and a reason to follow. We wrote about this in social media for beach clubs in Thailand.

Results From Thailand

We generated 2.6M Instagram reach for Alexa Beach Club Pattaya, 3.5x follower growth plus 5 Hoscar Awards for Nomads Hostel, and helped make Bamboo Beach Club the #1 beach club in Krabi.

Who This Is For

Brands whose social presence doesn't match the quality of their product. Hotels, resorts, villas, beach clubs, restaurants, bars, real estate developers and lifestyle brands in Pattaya, Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi and across Thailand.

Want organic content to turn into enquiries faster? Pair it with performance marketing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn. We recommend the platforms that matter for your audience rather than posting everywhere.

How many posts per month are included?

It depends on the package. A typical monthly plan includes a mix of reels, carousels or static posts and daily or weekly stories, agreed in your content calendar before each month starts.

Do you create the content or do we supply it?

Our in-house team produces the content — reels, graphics, captions and AI-assisted visuals. On-location shoots are scheduled as needed and can be included or billed separately depending on the package.

Do you post in Thai and English?

Yes. Captions and content are written in Thai, English or both, depending on who you are trying to reach.

Do you reply to comments and DMs?

Yes, community management is included. Booking and sales enquiries are passed to your team on WhatsApp or LINE so nothing gets lost.

Do you work with brands outside Pattaya?

Yes. We manage social media for brands in Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi and elsewhere in Thailand, as well as brands in India.
